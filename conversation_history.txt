I would like to make a web based browser game called "Gwen Mini Games". It should be a combination of minecraft and steal a brainrot. you use your powers to protect your brainrots.

----------

Built a playable “Gwen Mini Games” canvas prototype: blocky arena, protect wandering brainrots from incoming thieves with mining and powers. Key updates:

index.html: Canvas + HUD for score/energy/brainrots, control cheatsheet, themed title bar.
style.css: Dark radial backdrop, chunky card UI, pixel-esque borders for a Minecraft-ish vibe.
main.js: Core loop with player movement (WASD/arrows), thieves that grab and escape with brainrots, crystals to harvest energy/crystal charges, shockwave (Space) to stun/knock off carriers, wall placement (E) to block paths, scoring, pacing, and HUD wiring. Reset with R.
Run by opening index.html in a browser (or python3 -m http.server from the folder and visit http://localhost:8000). I haven’t been able to live-test here, so give it a quick manual run; shout if you want balance tweaks or additional mini-game ideas.
